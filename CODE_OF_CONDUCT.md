# Code of conduct

The project welcomes good-faith technical disagreement and contributions from people with different experience and backgrounds.

- Critique proposals and evidence, not people’s identity or personal circumstances.
- Make space for questions, corrections, and different technical approaches.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest when proposing decisions.
- Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, publish private information, or spam.
- Do not claim another person or organization endorses the project without their consent.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or close discussions to protect contributors. When safe, they should explain the reason and allow a response. Technical criticism is not misconduct by itself.

To report a concern without exposing private details, open a minimal issue asking for a private maintainer contact. Include no sensitive evidence until a private channel has been arranged. A future governance proposal should establish a dedicated reporting and appeal process; none is claimed to exist today.
