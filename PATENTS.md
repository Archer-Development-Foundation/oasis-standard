# Patent-policy status

**Status:** Initial policy disclosure; specification-specific policy not yet adopted.

The repository is licensed under [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). Section 3 contains a contributor patent grant with its stated scope and conditions. The license text controls; this note does not expand that grant.

The project has not adopted a separate standards-essential-patent policy, collected organization-wide royalty-free commitments, or established a patent-exclusion process. A copyright license and an openly accessible repository do not by themselves establish those commitments. No statement here guarantees that implementing the draft is free from third-party patent claims.

Before ratification, a public policy proposal must address:

- The normative scope covered and which participants can bind which rights.
- The form and timing of commitments, disclosures, exclusions, and withdrawals.
- Treatment of amendments, dependent profiles, and third-party technology.
- Public records, dispute handling, and the relationship to contribution licensing.

Existing standards-body policies can inform that proposal, but are not incorporated or claimed to apply merely by reference. See [governance](GOVERNANCE.md) and the [Apache license source](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
